Attendance: how it works
One link in the Zoom chat. Attendance is recorded and emailed automatically.
Every Mondayon Zoom
12:55 – 2:00 PMregistration open

Moderator

One step

At 12:55, paste this link in the Zoom chat and say “Please register your attendance now.”

academic.patientflowms.com

Optional: post it again at about 1:45 for late joiners. Nothing else to do.

Attendees

15 seconds

  1. Tap the link in the Zoom chat (or WhatsApp group).
  2. Sign in with Google.
  3. Name, Staff ID, level (first time only).
  4. Tap Register attendance.

Works from home or hospital. Once per person, Monday 12:55–2:00 PM only.

Heads & Secretary

Nothing to do

The attendance PDF report arrives by email at about 2:30 PM every Monday, automatically, to everyone on the list.

A monthly and a yearly summary are emailed automatically too.

2Common questions

  • Someone attended but could not register? Dashboard → + Manual entry. It is marked “Manual” in the report.
  • “Not open yet” or “Registration closed”? Registration only works on Monday from 12:55 to 2:00 PM.
  • Wrong name or ID? Dashboard → tap ✎ on that row, correct it, write a reason. Every change is logged.
  • Add or remove an email recipient, or change the time? Contact the system owner, Dr. Swaid Saulat (swsaulat@gmail.com).
